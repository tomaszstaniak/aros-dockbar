AROS x86_64 mainline (ABIv1). Tested under QEMU on mainline AROS of
2026-09-23 and on mainline AROS built from the sources of 2026-10-26. On a
system with window composition (mainline from 2026-10-24) and a 32-bit
screen, see-through docks are truly transparent; elsewhere they show a
picture of the desktop. Other systems are not tested. This package includes
Weather (Widgets drawer); it needs a running TCP/IP stack for its data.
