Dockbar @VERSION@ - contents and origin
=======================================

Dockbar program, icons (.info) and the SVG files in assets/
  Original work written for this project. Licence not decided yet
  (MIT has been proposed, not adopted). No artwork or code from other
  docks or from the AROS system icons is included.

  @BUILD@
  It uses the system's icon.library, intuition, graphics, workbench,
  cybergraphics and Zune (muimaster) libraries; none of them is included
  or changed.
