AROS for Raspberry Pi (aarch64, mainline ABIv1). Tested only under QEMU
(raspi3b) on the nightly of 2026-10-29, not on a real Raspberry Pi.
The package has libs/svgicon.library built for this target and
includes Weather (Widgets drawer); Weather needs a running TCP/IP stack
for its data. See-through docks are composed on this
system, as on mainline AROS for PC. The rest of this ReadMe also
describes the x86_64 packages.
